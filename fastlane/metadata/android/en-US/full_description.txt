ZzzTimer helps you fall asleep while listening to music or podcasts. When the timer ends, the volume is gradually lowered, playback is paused, then the volume is restored.

<ul>
<li>Start the default timer from a Quick Settings tile, or any duration from the app.</li>
<li>Extend, reduce or stop it from its notification, shown as a Live Update on Android 16 and later.</li>
<li>Dismissing the notification cancels the timer.</li>
<li>Optionally rewinds playback to where the volume started to fade, so you pick up where you left off.</li>
<li>Configurable default duration and steps, fade out duration, light and dark themes, Material You colors.</li>
<li>Automation with Tasker or adb, which can be turned off.</li>
<li>Optional diagnostics log, which only leaves your device if you export it.</li>
</ul>

No Internet permission, no ads, no tracking: nothing ever leaves your device.
